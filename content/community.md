## Contributing

Fly follows the [Open Source Guides](https://opensource.guide/) and welcomes all
kinds of contributions — code, documentation, bug reports, and ideas.

1. Read the [Code of Conduct](/code-of-conduct/) before participating.
2. [Open an issue](https://github.com/fly-lang/fly/issues/new) to report a bug or request a feature.
3. [Fork the repository](https://github.com/fly-lang/fly/fork) and send a pull request.
4. Follow [Git commit best practices](https://github.com/trein/dev-best-practices/wiki/Git-Commit-Best-Practices).

## Contact
