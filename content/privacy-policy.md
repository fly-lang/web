## Log Data

When you visit this website, we receive your IP address as part of our standard server logs.
We retain these logs for 3 months and do not use your data for any other purpose.

## Cookies

We use cookies for the Google Analytics service to:

- Remember what a user has done on previous pages.
- Distinguish unique users.
- Throttle the request rate.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.
